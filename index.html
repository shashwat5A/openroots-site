Why we started

OpenRoots started with a plant we'd grown up around but never really thought about: a root dug up for an upset stomach, long before "adaptogen" was a word anyone used. The remedy was always there. We just hadn't paid attention to it.

We wanted to bring that same instinct indoors. Whole roots and herbs, chosen for what they actually do, not how they photograph. No proprietary blends, no fillers, no claims we can't stand behind. Six teas, each built around one outcome, steeped the way they were meant to be.

What makes it different

Built around outcomes, not flavours

Every blend starts with a single goal (calm, sleep, digestion) and the herbs are chosen to match it, not the other way round.

Whole roots, nothing hiding behind them

No flavour dust, no "natural flavouring," no fillers bulking out the pouch. If it's in the blend, it's on the label.

Grown and packed close to source

Small batches, sourced from Indian growers, packed close to where the plants are grown rather than shipped and relabelled.
