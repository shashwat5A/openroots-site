Before it had a name

Our Story

Chapter 1 / 4

Little packets from home

Before Open Roots had a name, it came in little packets from home.

Eight years ago, two of us met at university. Between late-night assignments, exam stress and figuring out life away from home, there was one thing that always made its way back from home.

Little packets of tea, made by his mum.

She’d take everyday ingredients like tulsi, ginger and other herbs, blend them with green tea, and pack them into small portions. Some for stressful weeks, some for winding down after long days, and others for when he wasn’t feeling his best.

All he had to do was boil some water and make himself a cup.

Chapter 2 / 4

A recipe passed down

The lovely part? She wasn’t following a recipe she’d found online. She was doing what her own mother had taught her, using ingredients and combinations that had been part of their family for generations.

Back then, none of us imagined those little packets would turn into anything more. It was simply a mum looking after her son, even from miles away.

But over the years, as life got busier, we kept coming back to those blends.

Chapter 3 / 4

Why not for everyone?

We realised that while our lifestyles had changed, the little things that helped us feel cared for hadn’t. We still wanted those familiar ingredients and comforting rituals, just without having to gather, measure and mix everything ourselves.

And that’s when we thought: why not make this for everyone?

That’s how the three of us came together to build Open Roots.

A mother bringing years of knowledge and family recipes. And two of us who’d grown up with those blends, finding a way to make them part of modern life.

Chapter 4 / 4

Why “Open Roots”

We called it Open Roots for a reason.

Open, because we believe you should know exactly what goes into your cup.

Roots, because everything we do is inspired by where it all began: family, nature and traditions worth carrying forward.

Today, we’re taking those familiar ingredients and creating teas that are simple to make, easy to enjoy and thoughtfully blended.

Funny how something as small as a packet of tea can turn into something so much bigger.
